1. Aims & Symbolism

This product is not a generic bonsai night light — it is a Tree of Life piece drawn from Lehi’s dream in the Book of Mormon. Core symbols to decide whether to cast (or only imply):

Tree + white fruit

Love of God; fruit often read as blessings of the Atonement. Fruit = warm-white LEDs is the natural product mapping.[1][2]

Iron rod

Word of God — a metallic line people cling to. Easy as a straight copper/steel rod or thick wire beside a path.[1][3]

Strait path + mist

Path to the tree; mist = temptation. Path reads well in a landscape slab; mist can be tinted resin or omitted.[1]

River / building

Filthy river and great & spacious building complete the dream but clutter a small cast. Optional silhouettes on slab only.[1][4]

Design thesis: The slab wins if you want the dream to be readable as a scene (path → rod → tree → fruit). The cylinder wins if you want a classic glowing centerpiece where the tree itself is the whole story. Mock both in cardboard before mixing epoxy.

4. Physical Mockup Steps (before epoxy)

  1. Print the wire elevation (and the form silhouette you want) at roughly real size.
  2. Cut cardboard to the outer shape: rectangle for slab, or roll a tube for cylinder OD.
  3. Bend a cheap copper sample (or even bare craft wire) on the print — trunk first, then branches, mark LED spots with dots of paint or sticky putty.
  4. Tape fairy-light LEDs on the fruit numbers; power on in a dark room. Decide: enough glow? too Christmas-y? need fewer brighter points?
  5. For slab: add a cardboard “rod” and path strip; check whether the scene reads at arm’s length without labels.
  6. For cylinder: spin the tube — does the canopy look dead from the back? Add wrap-around branches if yes.
  7. Photograph both on a shelf next to a book / candle for scale. Pick a winner (or keep both SKUs).
  8. Only then cut “good” copper and pour Wisebond / Superclear / Alumilite per the parent plan.

6. Recommendation

Agree with the slab lean for a Book of Mormon Tree of Life: the dream is a journey scene, and a landscape plaque lets the iron rod and path earn their keep. The cylinder is still worth one cardboard + one small pour — it may win as a secondary “night light” SKU or gift for kids’ rooms.

Mockup order this weekend:

  3. Pour the winner first in Alumilite 24 oz (or Wisebond if you already bought it).

Symbolism minimum viable product: copper tree + glowing white fruit + (slab only) iron rod and path. River and spacious building are v2 set dressing.
