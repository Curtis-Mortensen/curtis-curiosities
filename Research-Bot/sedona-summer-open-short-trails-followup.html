Follow-Up: After Chapel Rock — Open Trails ≤4 Miles With the Best Views (and Any Caves Still Reachable)

Companion to the summer shade/cave report. Constraints: not the Chapel corridor, respect Pocket Fire closures (Fay / Boynton / Soldier Pass out), overcast + light drizzle, ≤4 miles round trip, prioritize views; caves if still open.

1. Aims

Answer a same-day follow-up after completing Chapel Rock / Chapel of the Holy Cross area hiking:

  1. Which open trails are ≤4 miles round trip with the strongest views?
  2. Which are geographically not the same south-179 / Chapel neighborhood?
  3. Are any caves still open given Fay, Boynton, and Soldier Pass closures?
  4. What changes because it is overcast and lightly drizzling (less heat, more slip risk)?

2. Hard Constraints From Closures

Treat as out for this decision: Boynton Canyon / Subway Cave, Fay Canyon / Arch, Soldier Pass Cave, Long Canyon / Birthing Cave access (Dry Creek Rd × Long Canyon Rd closures), Sugarloaf / Thunder Mountain approaches to Keyhole, Bear Mountain / Doe Mountain corridor on Boynton Pass, West Fork / Call of the Canyon, and related northwest Dry Creek access points.[1][2][3][4]

Repeatedly listed open: Cathedral Rock, Bell Rock, Chapel (already done), Crescent Moon / Red Rock Crossing / Buddha Beach, Munds Mountain Wilderness / Merry-Go-Round / Secret Slickrock, Airport Mesa area.[5][4]

Always re-check Visit Sedona Pocket Fire and the Coconino NF closure order before leaving the parking lot — orders change.[6]

3. Drizzle Changes the Ranking

Heat is no longer the limiter. Wet sandstone is. Prefer trails with hiking tread over polished slickrock scrambles. Cathedral Rock’s famous climb and any cave ledge (Robbers Roost) become meaningfully riskier in light rain.[7]

4. Go-Now Verdict

Best pick right now: Airport Mesa Loop (~3.3–3.5 mi) — different area (west/central Sedona off Airport Rd), continuously strong red-rock panoramas, under 4 miles, outside the Pocket Fire northwest closure cluster.[8][9]

If you want shorter than a full loop: park for the Airport / Sedona View overlook and do the summit spur + a short rim walk (well under 4 mi) for nearly the same view payoff.[10]

Only realistic open cave ≤4 mi: Raven Caves (~0.5 mi RT) off SR-179 south of town — likely outside the fire closure. Caveat: it is still the south-179 corridor (near Chapel geographically), and wet rock + drop-offs mean go slow or skip if the drizzle intensifies.[11]

Honest cave news: Subway, Birthing, Soldier Pass, and Keyhole are tied to closed trailheads. There is no remaining “main attraction” cave that is both open and far from the Chapel area under 4 miles.[1][3]

5. Ranked Options (≤4 mi, open-pattern, views first)

1. Airport Mesa Loop — TOP PICK

~3.3–3.5 mi loop · ~400–535 ft gain · Airport Road, west/central Sedona · different area from Chapel

Circles the mesa with continuous views of Cathedral, Capitol Butte, Wilson Mountain, Oak Creek Valley, and West Sedona.[8] Overcast is fine (little shade needed). Rocky tread — watch footing if rain thickens. Parking is small; overflow via airport viewpoint lot / Sedona View connector.[10]

2. Crescent Moon / Buddha Beach / Red Rock Crossing — BEST EASY VIEWS

Flexible under 4 mi · creek + classic Cathedral from below · south-west of town, not Chapel Ridge

Open-pattern day-use / creek walks with the postcard Cathedral Rock reflection views. Ideal in drizzle: no mandatory slickrock scramble, water nearby, still a different composition from Chapel overlooks.[5][4] Fee site (Coconino Pass / concessionaire rules apply at Crescent Moon).[12]

3. Cathedral Rock Trail — VIEWS YES / DRIZZLE CAUTION

~1.2 mi RT common scramble · Back O’ Beyond trailhead · different formation than Chapel

Best short “summit-ish” view hike still open — but polished sandstone gets slick when wet. If drizzle is more than mist, demote this below Airport Mesa and Crescent Moon.[5]

4. Raven Caves — ONLY OPEN CAVE CANDIDATE

~0.5 mi RT · SR-179 roadside pullout ~2 miles south of Sedona · alcove/passage network above Oak Creek

Short, genuinely cave-like passages and creek windows.[11] Not in the northwest fire-closure cluster. Tradeoff: geographically near the Chapel/179 south corridor you just used. Exposure and wet rock argue for turning around if ledges feel slick; bring a small light for darker passages.[11]

5. Merry-Go-Round / Secret Slickrock (east Schnebly / Munds) — IF ACCESS CONFIRMED TODAY

Under 4 mi only with high-clearance drive up Schnebly; ~6+ mi RT from paved Munds Wagon trailhead

July visitor resources list Munds / Merry-Go-Round / Secret Slickrock among open areas.[5] From paved parking, Munds Wagon to Merry-Go-Round is ~6.1–6.5 mi RT — over your 4-mile cap.[13] Only use this now if you can drive closer (Cow Pies / near-rock parking) and keep the walk short.[14]

7. Suggested Decision Tree for the Next Hour

  1. Want the best open views in a new area under 4 mi? → Airport Mesa (full loop if energy; overlook + spur if not).
  2. Want easy + pretty in drizzle? → Crescent Moon / Buddha Beach for Cathedral-from-the-creek.
  3. Must have a cave today? → Raven Caves only — accept the south-179 proximity; abort if ledges are wet.
  4. Do not burn the afternoon hunting Robbers Roost / FR 525C without confirming that forest-road access is open; northwest dirt-road corridors have been repeatedly restricted during Pocket Fire response.[3][2]

8. Limitations

  • Closure polygons change; this follow-up synthesizes mid/late July 2026 visitor + local reporting, not a live Forest Service GIS ping at trailhead time.
  • “Chapel Rock” is interpreted as the Chapel of the Holy Cross / Chapel Trail south-Sedona experience the party just finished.
  • Raven Caves is a social/use trail with exposure; it is a cave option, not a USFS interpretive hike.

9. Conclusion

With Fay / Boynton / Soldier (and Birthing / Keyhole access) out, the best ≤4-mile view hike in a different area is Airport Mesa. Pair or substitute Crescent Moon / Buddha Beach if you want easier footing in the drizzle. For caves, expectations must shrink: Raven Caves is the remaining short open candidate, with the geographic tradeoff that it sits on the same south-179 approach you already used.
